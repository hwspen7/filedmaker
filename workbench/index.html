Three steps to model a venue

1. Open a practice case or use Bowl to generate an editable seating layout.

2. Use 2D Grid and Snap to position sections. Alignment lines appear as you drag.

3. In 3D, drag a component directly to move it; Alt-drag to change height, or Shift-drag for finer placement. Drag empty space to orbit the camera.
